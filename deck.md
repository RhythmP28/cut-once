# Cut Once

We are going to fix how the world builds.

Every building starts as a 3D idea, yet crews still build from flat drawings. Since 2001, Canadian homebuilding has become 37% less productive per worker while the wider business sector grew. In the U.S., an estimated $177 billion a year in labor goes to fixing mistakes, hunting for project data, and resolving conflicts. And it isn't just the pros: 58% of surveyed homeowners went over budget on DIY projects because of errors.

Cut Once puts the plan on the site. On a Meta Quest 3, the blueprint appears in the real space as a true-scale hologram: walls, framing, scaffolding, exactly where they'll go, before anyone makes a cut. Using Elasticsearch to search the plan, materials, and building guidance, Cut Once labels whatever you look at with its dimensions, material, and time to build. Ask "how long will this take?" or "what goes here?" and OpenAI's models answer in context, spoken back through ElevenLabs so your hands stay free.

Whether you're a foreman walking a site or a first-timer building a treehouse, Cut Once catches the mistake while it's still a hologram, not lumber. Then it turns the plan into a cut list and materials order you can send straight to the supplier.
